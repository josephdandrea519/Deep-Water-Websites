What to realistically expect from SEO

SEO isn't instant — but it's the only marketing channel that gets cheaper and more effective over time. Here's an honest timeline.

Weeks 1–4
Getting Listed
Your site is submitted to Google and set up so its crawler can read every page. Google starts checking you out, and you show up when someone searches your exact business name.
Months 2–3
Local Visibility Builds
Your Google listing and the code that tells Google where you are based start putting you in "near me" searches. You begin appearing on the map for your city.
Months 3–6
Keyword Rankings Take Hold
Your individual service pages start ranking for the searches you care about. Visitors start arriving from Google — slowly at first, then noticeably. It also becomes clear which competitors are still ahead of you, and why.
Months 6–12
Traffic Compounds
Your rankings settle in, more people click through when they see you, and Google starts treating your site as a trusted source. New leads arrive consistently without any ad spend. Most clients see 2–4× more inbound inquiries.
Year 1+
Established Authority
You own your local market for the searches that bring ready-to-buy customers. Competitors are chasing you. Your site brings in work instead of just sitting there.
Unlike ads, SEO compounds. Every month your site is optimized, it gets harder for competitors to displace you — and your cost-per-lead keeps dropping.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What if I don't have a domain or hosting yet? +
No problem. I'll walk you through getting a domain and set up hosting for you. Most of my clients use Netlify (free for static sites) or Namecheap for domains. I handle all of it.
How long does a website take to build? +
Typically about 2 weeks depending on the package. Some simpler builds move faster when you have your content ready upfront.
Do you offer payment plans? +
Yes. For one-time builds I typically structure a 50% deposit upfront and 50% on delivery. If you would rather avoid a large upfront cost, the Website as a Service plan covers everything for one flat monthly rate. Just mention it when you reach out and we will figure out what works.
Will my site show up on Google? +
Yes. Every site I build is registered with Google and set up so it can be found and listed the moment it goes live — that's included, never an add-on. Growth and Premium packages also include ongoing work on the specific searches you want to win.
Can I update the site myself after it's built? +
Absolutely. I can build on a platform you can manage yourself (like Squarespace or WordPress), or I can handle all updates on a monthly retainer. Your call.
What industries do you work with? +
I work with any local service business — HVAC, insurance, real estate, contractors, restaurants, medical practices, you name it. If you serve customers in a local area, I can build a site that gets you found.
